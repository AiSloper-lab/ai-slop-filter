AI Slop Filter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

AI Slop Filter ("the extension") hides AI-generated videos on YouTube. It is built to work entirely inside your browser. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What the extension stores

  • Your settings: on/off switches, keywords, and blocked and trusted channels. These are saved with your browser's built-in storage (storage.sync). If you have browser sync turned on, your browser (Google, Mozilla, Opera or Microsoft) may sync them between your own devices under its own privacy policy.
  • A local cache of video IDs that records whether each video carries YouTube's AI label, plus a counter of how many videos were filtered. These stay on your device (storage.local), are deleted automatically after 30 days, and can be cleared at any time from the Settings page.

Network requests

To read YouTube's "Altered or synthetic content" disclosure, the extension loads the public YouTube page of videos shown in your feed. These requests go only from your browser to youtube.com, the same as when you visit the site yourself. The extension does not contact any other server, and it has no server of its own.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No collection of browsing history, watch history, account information or personal data.
  • No selling or transferring of data to anyone.

Permissions

  • storage saves your settings and the local cache described above.
  • Access to youtube.com lets the extension read video titles and channel names on YouTube pages, hide or blur AI videos, and check YouTube's AI label.

Changes
